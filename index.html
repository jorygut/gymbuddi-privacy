Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to suggest something? Email jorygut@gmail.com and you'll get a reply.

It helps to include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

My workouts aren't syncing between devices. Open Profile → Sync & Backup. If it says "On this iPhone only", the line underneath explains why — usually you're not signed in to iCloud, or iCloud Drive is turned off in Settings. The app works fully either way; it just won't sync.

How do I back up my data? Profile → Sync & Backup → Save Backup exports everything as a JSON file you keep. iCloud syncs deletions too, so a backup is the copy that survives a mistake.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app to remove it from your device. To also clear the synced copy, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → GymBuddi → Delete Data.

Privacy Policy

The short version. GymBuddi does not collect your data. There are no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party services of any kind. The app makes no network requests to any server operated by the developer, because there is no such server. Your training data stays on your iPhone, and if you have iCloud enabled it syncs to your own private iCloud storage, which only you can read.

What the app stores

GymBuddi stores the information you enter so it can show it back to you:

  • Exercises you create, and any notes you write about them
  • Workouts, including the sets, weights, reps and timings you log
  • Workout plans and their days
  • Bodyweight readings you record
  • Weekly training targets and app settings, such as your preferred units

Where it is stored

On your device. All of the above is written to a database on your iPhone.

In your private iCloud, if iCloud is available. When you are signed in to iCloud, GymBuddi mirrors that same data to the private database of its iCloud container, so it is available on your other devices and survives replacing your phone.

This is Apple's CloudKit private database. It lives in your personal iCloud account, it counts against your iCloud storage, and it is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer of GymBuddi cannot read, query, export or delete anything in it. No part of your data is ever sent to the developer or to anyone else.

You can see which mode the app is in at any time under Profile → Sync & Backup. If iCloud is unavailable the app works exactly the same, just without syncing.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics or usage measurement of any kind
  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or ad networks
  • No tracking, and no sharing of data with other companies
  • No third-party SDKs or libraries — the app has no external dependencies
  • No account, login, email address, or password
  • No access to your location, camera, microphone, photos, or contacts
  • No access to Apple Health

Notifications

If you allow notifications, GymBuddi uses them only to tell you when a rest timer has finished. These are scheduled and delivered locally by your iPhone. No notification is ever sent from a server, and declining them leaves every other feature working.

The app also uses silent background notifications from Apple as part of iCloud syncing. These carry no content and exist only to tell the app that your own data changed on another device.

Backups you create

Profile → Sync & Backup can export your data as a JSON file. That file is produced on your device and goes wherever you choose to save it. The developer never receives a copy. Anyone you share the file with can read its contents, so treat it as you would any personal document.

Deleting your data

Deleting GymBuddi from your iPhone removes the copy stored on that device.

To also remove the synced copy from your iCloud account, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → GymBuddi → Delete Data. This is permanent, and because the developer has no access to your data, it cannot be recovered for you afterwards.

Children

GymBuddi is a general-purpose fitness log. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.
